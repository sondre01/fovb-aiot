Integrated Hardware & Sensors

The Four-in-One Vital Signs & AI Ecosystem

The FOVB-AIoT platform synchronizes medical-grade sensors, dual microcontrollers, and computer vision into an automated, high-precision health screening station.

Sensor 01

Body Temperature

MLX90614 Infrared Sensor

Non-contact medical infrared sensor providing hygienic, accurate measurement of core body temperature without physical skin contact, preventing cross-contamination.

Accuracy: ±0.2 °C Zero-touch safety
Sensor 02

Pulse & Respiratory Rate

MAX30102 PPG Sensor

Utilizes photoplethysmography (PPG) to measure Heart Rate (BPM), blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2 %), and derived Respiratory Rate simultaneously.

SpO2 Range: 70% – 100% Real-time wave tracking
Sensor 03

Blood Pressure with AI

YOLO Computer Vision + HD Camera

Digital cuff unit integrated with an HD camera running YOLO object detection on the Mini PC to accurately read systolic and diastolic values from the LCD display.

AI Automated Reading Eliminates manual logging
Sensor 04

LiDAR Height & Weight (BMI)

TF-Luna LiDAR + HX711 Load Cell

Laser time-of-flight LiDAR sensor measures exact height overhead, while industrial load cells compute precise weight. Calculates BMI instantly (WHO Asia-Pacific).

Instant BMI calculation High-precision laser pulses
Security & ID

Fast-Track RFID Tap

RC522 RFID 13.56 MHz Reader

Tap your RTU Student / Employee ID card or RFID tag to immediately pull up your patient profile without tedious keyboard typing or line delays.

Instant identification Secure contactless token
Central Hub

Mini PC & Cloud IoT

Dell Mini PC + Arduino Mega & Nano

Dual Arduino microcontrollers coordinate sensor telemetry and transmit it to the Intel Core i5 Mini PC for real-time cloud sync and thermal slip printing.

Thermal receipt printing Real-time cloud sync
Kiosk Workflow

How the FOVB-AIoT Kiosk Works

From contactless RFID tap to AI health risk prediction in 4 seamless steps at the RTU Pasig Clinic.

1

1. Register & Tap RFID

Sign up online through this portal to register your RTU Student ID and RFID tag. Tap your card on the kiosk reader to begin your session.

2

2. Stand for Height & Weight

Step onto the platform. The TF-Luna LiDAR sensor measures height from above while the load cells weigh you to instantly calculate your BMI.

3

3. Scan Temperature & Vitals

Place forehead near the MLX90614 infrared sensor, insert finger into the pulse oximeter, and place arm in the AI-monitored BP cuff.

4

4. AI Risk Score & Slip

Receive your printed thermal slip, review your AI Comprehensive Health Risk Score, and access long-term trend charts on your online dashboard.

Health Standards

Clinical Normal Ranges & Reference Guide

Based on Department of Health (DOH) Philippines & World Health Organization (WHO) medical guidelines.

Body Temperature

Target: 36.5 – 37.5 °C

Normal range indicates proper metabolic function and no acute infection or febrile illness.

Heart Rate (Pulse)

Target: 60 – 100 BPM

Resting heart rate reflects cardiovascular efficiency. Elevated rates may indicate stress, fever, or tachycardia.

Respiratory Rate

Target: 12 – 20 breaths/min

Monitors pulmonary ventilation. Rapid breathing can signify respiratory exertion or underlying conditions.

Blood Pressure

Target: < 120 / < 80 mmHg

Systolic & diastolic pressure against arterial walls. Critical for early screening of hypertension.

Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)

Target: 95% – 100%

Percentage of oxygenated hemoglobin in arterial blood. Values below 95% warrant medical evaluation.

Body Mass Index (BMI)

Target: 18.5 – 22.9 kg/m²

Asia-Pacific WHO metric assessing healthy weight distribution for height to screen obesity and malnutrition.
