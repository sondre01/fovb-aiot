Patient & User Manual

How to Use the FOVB-AIoT Kiosk

Follow this quick step-by-step guide for a safe, hygienic, and precise vital signs screening at RTU Pasig Clinic.

Before You Begin Your Screening

For the most accurate physiological readings, please rest for 3–5 minutes if you just walked up the stairs. Avoid heavy exercise or consuming high-caffeine energy drinks immediately prior to taking your Blood Pressure and Heart Rate tests.

1
Phase 1: Authentication

Tap Your RTU RFID Card

Approach the FOVB-AIoT kiosk and hold your RTU Student ID / Employee RFID card against the labeled RFID Reader zone (RC522) on the front console.

  • If already registered, your name, student number, and previous records will appear on screen.
  • If you haven't linked your RFID card yet, sign up online first or input your Student ID.
2
Phase 2: Anthropometrics

Stand Upright for Height, Weight & BMI

Step onto the center of the base weight scale platform. Stand upright with your head level and back straight.

  • The overhead TF-Luna LiDAR laser sensor will measure your height in seconds.
  • The four precision load cells determine your weight and auto-compute your Asia-Pacific BMI.
3
Phase 3: Thermometry

Non-Contact Body Temperature

Align your forehead 2 to 4 centimeters in front of the MLX90614 infrared optical sensor.

  • No physical touching is required, ensuring 100% clinic hygiene.
  • The kiosk will emit an audio confirmation once your core temperature is locked in.
4
Phase 4: Pulse & Oxygen

Insert Index Finger in Pulse Oximeter

Gently place your index finger onto the MAX30102 optical PPG sensor inside the finger cradle.

  • Breathe normally and remain still for 10–15 seconds while the photoplethysmogram tracks your arterial pulse.
  • Heart Rate (BPM), SpO2 Oxygen Saturation (%), and Respiratory Rhythm will be recorded.
5
Phase 5: Cardiovascular

Blood Pressure with AI Camera Reading

Rest your left arm comfortably in the blood pressure cuff at heart level. Press the green start button on the BP module.

  • Relax your muscles, do not speak or clench your fist during the cuff inflation.
  • The integrated HD Camera + YOLO AI Vision model will automatically detect and extract the Systolic and Diastolic values from the digital display.

Session Completion & Your Results

What happens right after your screening:

Thermal Printed Slip

The kiosk prints a pocket-sized health receipt containing all your vital readings, BMI, and date stamp for your personal records or clinic visit.

Real-time Cloud Sync

Your session is immediately synced to your online account on this website. You can view longitudinal health trends and download official slips.

RTU Clinic Consultation

If your AI Risk Score is Moderate or High, present your slip or digital pass to the registered nurse on duty at RTU Pasig Clinic for medical advice.

Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register my RFID card if I don't have an account yet?

You can create an account in 1 minute using the Online Sign Up page. You can enter your RFID card UID directly, or click "Auto-Assign RFID UID" to test. You can also ask the RTU Pasig Clinic staff to assist with linking your physical student ID tag.

Can I still use the system if I forgot my physical RFID card?

Yes! You can manually sign in on the touchscreen using your RTU Student/Employee ID or Email address. The RFID card is designed for maximum speed and zero-touch convenience.

How does the AI Comprehensive Risk Score work?

The FOVB-AIoT AI risk engine evaluates all 6 parameters (Temperature, Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, Respiratory Rate, Oxygen Saturation, and BMI) simultaneously against established World Health Organization (WHO) and DOH clinical benchmarks to detect anomalies and categorize overall risk into Low, Moderate, or High.

Is my personal health data kept safe and confidential?

Yes. All patient records in the FOVB-AIoT system are encrypted and accessible exclusively by the student/faculty account owner and authorized RTU Pasig Clinic medical staff in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
