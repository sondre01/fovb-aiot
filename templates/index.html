Physical Kiosk Technology

The Physical FOVB-AIoT Kiosk Architecture

The hardware sensors, dual microcontrollers, and computer vision are integrated into the physical standalone kiosk station deployed at the RTU Pasig Clinic. This website serves as the online documentation archive, USB RFID registration portal, and historical records dashboard.

Sensor 01

Body Temperature

MLX90614 Infrared Sensor

Non-contact medical infrared sensor providing hygienic, accurate measurement of core body temperature without physical skin contact, preventing cross-contamination in the campus clinic.

Accuracy: ±0.2 °C Zero-touch safety
Sensor 02

Pulse & Respiratory Rate

MAX30102 PPG Sensor

Utilizes photoplethysmography (PPG) to measure Heart Rate (BPM), blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2 %), and derived Respiratory Rate simultaneously through a comfortable finger cradle.

SpO2 Range: 70% – 100% Real-time wave tracking
Sensor 03

Blood Pressure with AI

YOLO Computer Vision + HD Camera

Digital cuff unit integrated with an HD camera running YOLO object detection on the Mini PC to accurately read systolic, diastolic, and pulse values from the LCD while filtering optical noise.

AI Automated Reading Eliminates manual logging
Sensor 04

LiDAR Height & Weight (BMI)

TF-Luna LiDAR + HX711 Load Cell

Laser time-of-flight LiDAR sensor measures exact height overhead, while industrial load cells compute precise weight. Calculates Body Mass Index (BMI) instantly according to WHO Asia-Pacific standards.

Instant BMI calculation High-precision laser pulses
Security & ID

Fast-Track RFID Tap

RC522 RFID 13.56 MHz Reader

Tap your RTU Student / Employee ID card or RFID tag to immediately pull up your patient profile. No tedious manual keyboard typing or kiosk line delays.

Instant identification Secure contactless token
Central Hub

Mini PC & Cloud IoT

Dell Mini PC + Arduino Mega & Nano

Dual Arduino microcontrollers coordinate sensor telemetry and send it to the Intel Core i5 Mini PC. Data is instantly stored in the clinic IoT web portal with thermal slip printing.

Thermal receipt printing Real-time cloud sync
Kiosk & Web Workflow

How the FOVB-AIoT System Works

From contactless screening at the campus clinic station to viewing your complete health history and digital slips on this portal.

1

1. Register with USB RFID

Register online through this portal using your laptop and USB RFID scanner (or manual entry). Your profile is securely linked to your RTU ID.

2

2. Stand for Height & Weight

Step onto the platform. The TF-Luna LiDAR sensor measures height from above while the load cells weigh you to instantly calculate your BMI.

3

3. Scan Temperature & Vitals

Place forehead near the MLX90614 infrared sensor, insert finger into the pulse oximeter, and place arm in the AI-monitored BP cuff.

4

4. Access History Online

Receive your printed slip at the clinic, then log in to this portal anytime to review your long-term health trends and past screening history.

Health Standards

Clinical Normal Ranges & Reference Guide

Based on Department of Health (DOH) Philippines & World Health Organization (WHO) medical guidelines.

Body Temperature

Target: 36.5 – 37.5 °C

Normal range indicates proper metabolic function and no acute infection or febrile illness.

Heart Rate (Pulse)

Target: 60 – 100 BPM

Resting heart rate reflects cardiovascular efficiency. Elevated rates may indicate stress, fever, or tachycardia.

Respiratory Rate

Target: 12 – 20 breaths/min

Monitors pulmonary ventilation. Rapid breathing can signify respiratory exertion or underlying conditions.

Blood Pressure

Target: < 120 / < 80 mmHg

Systolic & diastolic pressure against arterial walls. Critical for early screening of hypertension.

Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)

Target: 95% – 100%

Percentage of oxygenated hemoglobin in arterial blood. Values below 95% warrant medical evaluation.

Body Mass Index (BMI)

Target: 18.5 – 22.9 kg/m²

Asia-Pacific WHO metric assessing healthy weight distribution for height to screen obesity and malnutrition.
