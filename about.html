Undergraduate Capstone Thesis

FOVB-AIoT Research & Innovation

Four-in-One Vital Sign Sensors with BMI Calculation using AI and IoT for Health Risk Prediction in RTU Pasig Clinic.

Official Abstract
Rizal Technological University • March 2026

This study presents the development of the FOVB-AIoT, a kiosk-based health monitoring system that integrates a Four-in-One Vital Sign Sensor with Body Mass Index (BMI) calculation using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) for Health Risk Prediction. Designed to improve efficiency and accuracy over traditional manual screening methods in educational and institutional health clinics, the FOVB-AIoT platform measures key physiological parameters—including body temperature, heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation (SpO2), and blood pressure (via AI computer vision)—while simultaneously computing BMI using automated laser LiDAR height and digital load cell weight sensors.

An RFID-based fast-track system enables rapid authentication without repetitive manual data entry. Telemetry data are securely transmitted to an IoT web portal for real-time access by clinic personnel. The integrated AI engine analyzes vital sign patterns to detect anomalous physiological signs and calculate a Comprehensive Risk Score, providing actionable health recommendations and automated thermal slips to support proactive preventive care.

Keywords: FOVB-AIoT RTU Pasig Clinic AI Computer Vision (YOLO) Comprehensive Risk Score IoT Health Database RFID Authentication
System Architecture

Hardware & Software Integration

A multi-tier architecture uniting sensor telemetry, dual microcontrollers, AI vision, and web-based IoT storage.

1. Hardware & Microcontrollers

  • Dell Mini PC (Intel Core i5 10th Gen): Central processing unit managing real-time data processing, database sync, and AI risk prediction algorithms.
  • Arduino Mega 2560: Primary microcontroller managing data streams from the LiDAR, load cells, temperature, and pulse oximeter.
  • Arduino Nano: Secondary microcontroller dedicated to the custom blood pressure enclosure, controlling LCD feedback and LED indicators.
  • Fortress CF160TM 15.6" Touchscreen: High-definition capacitive interactive user interface for guided screening sessions.
  • Embedded Thermal Receipt Printer: Instantly issues printed clinical vital slips for patient records and physical clinic referrals.

2. Clinical Sensors & Peripherals

  • MLX90614 Infrared Sensor: Non-contact radiometric temperature sensor ensuring medical hygiene and fast reading.
  • MAX30102 PPG Sensor: Photoplethysmography sensor extracting Heart Rate, SpO2 blood oxygen, and respiratory rhythm.
  • TF-Luna LiDAR Sensor: Laser time-of-flight sensor overhead for accurate, contactless height detection.
  • HX711 & Load Cell Platform: Industrial analog-to-digital weight sensor converting mechanical force to accurate mass for BMI.
  • RC522 RFID 13.56 MHz Reader: Contactless smart card interface for 1-tap student/staff profile recognition.

3. AI Computer Vision & Cloud IoT

  • YOLO Object Detection & OCR: HD camera trained with YOLOv8 computer vision to recognize and read seven-segment digital blood pressure numbers while rejecting optical interference.
  • AI Comprehensive Risk Engine: Multi-vital inference engine weighting temperature, blood pressure, pulse, SpO2, and BMI into a 0-100% clinical risk index.
  • Flask / Client-Side IoT Engine: RESTful telemetry sync, automated Asia-Pacific BMI calculations, and patient authentication.
  • Responsive Web Dashboard: Real-time patient analytics, longitudinal trend graphs (Chart.js), and printable RTU clinic slips.
